## How to Actually Get Smarter From These

- **Don't read all of them linearly.** Do the Start Here list first, then pick one field per month.
- **Use the three-pass method** from Keshav's *How to Read a Paper*: skim (5 min), read without details (1 hr), then deep-read only the ones that matter.
- **Reimplement the ML papers.** Building a tiny transformer, ResNet, or diffusion model from scratch teaches more than reading ten times.
- **Write a one-page summary** of each paper in your own words: the problem, the key idea, why it worked, and what it got wrong.
- **Read the cross-field papers for transferable mental models**, such as emergence, incentives, bounded rationality, and the limits of formal systems. These tend to improve your thinking more than another incremental ML paper.
- **Follow citations backward.** When a paper moves you, read what it cites.

Since the field moves fast, supplement this with recent work from arXiv, the Anthropic, OpenAI, and DeepMind research pages, and annual survey papers. I can also turn this into a 12-month reading schedule or narrow it down to the 30 papers best suited for building AI systems.
