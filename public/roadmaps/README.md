# Roadmap sources

The four Markdown files in this directory preserve the complete user-supplied roadmaps. Their bullet points are represented in `src/expanded-curriculum.json` as learning steps, grouped by their original headings.

The Namaste DSA question titles, original categories, difficulty labels, practice links, lesson links, and article links were extracted from https://namastedev.com/namaste-dsa-sheet on 2026-10-19. The snapshot contains 165 questions across 14 categories. This is a snapshot, not a live feed; future source additions require updating the curriculum. Problem statements and solutions are linked rather than copied.

Research paper titles and author/year information are supplied by the user, whose source notes that some entries are from memory. Google Scholar search links are provided to find and verify the exact paper and edition. Reading lists retain repeated recommendations across the initial reading list and subject categories.
